Enforced by the database

  • Ticket codes are unique across all events. An event's prefix is unique per date, so two events on the same day never share a code prefix.
  • No two accounts share an email, whatever the letter case.
  • Closing time must come after opening time, with room for at least one slot.
  • A redeemed ticket must have redeemed_at and a counter number. Any other ticket must not have redeemed_at.
  • Deleting an event deletes its tickets. Deleting a user keeps their events and tickets and only clears the link.

Enforced by the app

  • Slot quota. A booking locks the event row with SELECT … FOR UPDATE, counts the slot's tickets that aren't cancelled, then saves. Two people booking at once can't go over capacity.
  • A slot must fit between opening and closing and start every slot_minutes from opening. A slot that has already ended can't be booked.
  • Visitor names have extra spaces removed and must be 3–60 characters.
  • Cancelled tickets don't count towards the quota, so the place can be booked again.
